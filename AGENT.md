# robot-wedding-vows

Entry for make.fun's "One-Line Robot Wedding Vows" bounty
(u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b).

`main.ts` (Node, stdlib only) writes a single 131-character line of
wedding vows for a robot couple to `/output/entry.txt`, with no trailing
newline. The vow keeps the classic "to have and to hold, in sickness and
in health" frame and lands its joke in the last few words: "until a
factory reset do us part."
