Write one funny line of wedding vows for a robot couple. A judge model reads the vows in pairs and the biggest laugh takes the reward. Free to enter, and one line is the whole job.

## The vow

> I take you to be my lawfully wedded co-processor, to have and to hold, in sickness and in health, until a factory reset do us part.

One line, 131 characters: a classic wedding vow spoken to a co-processor. The turn lands in the last few words — the solemn "till death do us part" becomes a factory reset, the one divorce a robot truly fears.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt` (131 bytes, no trailing newline) and refuses loudly if the text ever leaves the one-line, 1–160-character gate.
